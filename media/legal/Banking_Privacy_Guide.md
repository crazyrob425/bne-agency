# BANKING PRIVACY GUIDE FOR ADULT CREATORS
**PROTECTING FINANCIAL IDENTITY & MITIGATING DE-BANKING RISKS**

## INTRODUCTION
Adult content creators face a dual threat regarding financial privacy: the risk of doxxing (real names appearing on customer billing statements) and the risk of sudden "de-banking" (account closure) due to violation of strict Acceptable Use Policies (AUPs) by traditional financial institutions.

This guide outlines standard operational security measures for securing financial privacy and stability.

---

## 1. THE FOUNDATION: FORMING AN ANONYMOUS LLC
Operating under your legal name directly exposes you to buyers and restricts your privacy. The industry standard is operating through a Limited Liability Company (LLC).

- **Anonymous States:** Form your LLC in Wyoming, Delaware, or New Mexico. These states do not require the names of the LLC members to be published on the public state registry.
- **Registered Agent:** Hire a Commercial Registered Agent in that state. Their address will be the public-facing address for the LLC, keeping your home address private.
- **Employer Identification Number (EIN):** Apply for an EIN from the IRS. This functions as a social security number for the business, allowing you to open business bank accounts without handing out your personal SSN to platforms.

## 2. BANKING STRATEGIES TO AVOID DE-BANKING
Many major banks aggressively terminate accounts associated with the adult entertainment industry due to "reputational risk."

- **Business Checking:** Open a Business Checking Account using your LLC and EIN. Ensure the DBA (Doing Business As) is innocuous and does not reference adult entertainment.
- **Friendly Institutions:** Research and utilize credit unions or adult-friendly FinTech banks. Avoid transferring funds directly from major adult platforms to highly conservative regional banks.
- **Buffer Accounts:** Use a payment processor or business wallet as a buffer. Platform payouts go to the buffer account, and you manually transfer from the buffer to your primary savings. 

## 3. PROTECTING YOUR IDENTITY ON TRANSACTIONS
When customers purchase content from you directly, you must control the billing descriptor.

- **Billing Descriptor:** The billing descriptor is the text that appears on the buyer's credit card statement. If operating an LLC, the billing descriptor will be the LLC name, NOT your legal name.
- **Platform Payouts:** When receiving payouts from major platforms (e.g., OnlyFans, Fansly), ensure your W-9/W-8BEN is filed under your LLC's name and EIN. This ensures tax documents and payouts are legally tied to the corporate entity.

## 4. TAX PRIVACY & PREPARATION
Operating as a business provides tax advantages but requires strict bookkeeping.

- **Commingling:** Never commingle personal and business funds. Buy equipment, software, and props using the Business Account. Pay yourself a draw or salary to your Personal Account.
- **CPA Engagement:** Hire a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) familiar with digital creator taxation. A knowledgeable CPA will help maximize deductions (internet, equipment, home office, wardrobe) while ensuring compliance.

---
*Note: Financial regulations vary globally. U.S. creators are subject to IRS regulations regarding independent contractor income (1099-NEC). Always consult with a licensed CPA and attorney.*
