## Executive Overview

Pricing creator management services is fundamentally different from pricing traditional agency retainers. In the creator economy, revenue is variable, platform algorithms shift without warning, and audience behavior evolves rapidly. A fixed monthly retainer creates misaligned incentives: the agency gets paid whether your revenue grows or stagnates.

B.N.E. Studio's pricing model is designed to eliminate this misalignment. We treat our engagement as an investment, not a service contract. We deploy our proprietary systems, strategic expertise, and operational infrastructure into your brand, and our compensation is tied to the revenue growth we generate together. If your brand does not grow, we do not earn. This is not marketing language — it is the structural foundation of every B.N.E. Studio partnership.
