## What 18 U.S.C. § 2257 Requires

Federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75) mandates that any producer of sexually explicit digital content must:
1. Maintain picture identification and age verification documents for every performer depicted.
2. Affix an official compliance statement to all distributed content specifying the physical address of the designated Custodian of Records.
3. Produce auditable inspection records upon demand by federal law enforcement.

Failure to maintain § 2257 records can result in severe federal criminal penalties, asset forfeiture, and immediate platform bans.
