# ANONYMITY AUDIT WORKSHEET
**PRIVACY VULNERABILITY SELF-ASSESSMENT TOOL**

## 1. DIGITAL FOOTPRINT & HARDWARE
*Objective: Ensure separation of personal and professional hardware/networks.*

- [ ] **Dedicated Hardware:** Do you use a separate laptop/phone exclusively for content creation and business operations?
- [ ] **EXIF Data Stripping:** Are you actively stripping EXIF metadata (GPS location, device type, timestamp) from all photos and videos before uploading?
- [ ] **VPN Usage:** Is a zero-log Virtual Private Network (VPN) active 100% of the time on your business devices?
- [ ] **Dedicated Network:** Do you avoid connecting your professional devices to your personal home Wi-Fi or Bluetooth networks (which can link MAC addresses)?

## 2. ONLINE ACCOUNTS & COMMUNICATIONS
*Objective: Eliminate cross-contamination between personal accounts and professional alias.*

- [ ] **Burner Phone Number:** Do you use a secure VoIP or burner SIM (e.g., Mint Mobile, Google Voice, MySudo) for all business verifications and 2FA?
- [ ] **Alias Email Infrastructure:** Are all social media, hosting, and banking accounts registered to a dedicated encrypted email (e.g., ProtonMail) not linked to your legal name?
- [ ] **Username Sanitization:** Have you ensured your alias usernames have *never* been used in your personal life (e.g., gaming tags, old forums)?
- [ ] **Contact Syncing:** Are contact/address book syncing permissions strictly disabled on all alias social media accounts?

## 3. CONTENT & BACKGROUND SANITIZATION
*Objective: Remove identifying real-world markers from your visual content.*

- [ ] **Tattoos and Birthmarks:** Are identifying bodily markings covered or digitally blurred in content if you wish to remain unrecognized?
- [ ] **Background Elements:** Are identifying room features, window views, reflections in mirrors, or unique furniture removed from your filming area?
- [ ] **Audio Scrubbing:** Do you edit out identifying audio (e.g., background television, local sirens, calling out real names)?

## 4. FINANCIAL & LEGAL PRIVACY
*Objective: Protect your legal identity while maintaining commercial viability.*

- [ ] **Business Entity Formation:** Have you established an LLC (preferably in an anonymous state like Wyoming, Delaware, or New Mexico) to act as a buffer between your alias and your legal identity?
- [ ] **Registered Agent:** Do you use a third-party Commercial Registered Agent to keep your personal address off public corporate registries?
- [ ] **Banking Separation:** Do you have a dedicated Business Checking Account in the name of your LLC, completely separate from your personal finances?
- [ ] **Payment Processing:** When receiving payouts, are they routed to the LLC's EIN rather than your personal SSN?
