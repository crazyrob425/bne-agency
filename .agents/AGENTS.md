# Project Rules — B.N.E. Studio (Blacklisted Niche Entertainment)

## B.N.E. Studio — Content, Persona & GEO Directives

### 1. Brand Identity & Target Audience
- **Brand Entity:** Blacklisted Niche Entertainment (B.N.E.) Studio.
- **Industry Vertical:** Professional Talent Management, Marketing, and Monetization Infrastructure for adult entertainment creators, webcam models, and subscription-platform talent (OnlyFans, Fansly, clip stores, in-person companions).
- **Target Persona:** Female content creators (early 20s to late 40s) who are street-smart, self-made CEOs operating their own businesses.
- **Core Value Proposition:** Flat monthly retainers (0% revenue commission), 100% creator IP & account ownership, 18 U.S.C. § 2257 Custodian of Records representation, 24/7 DM monetization, and anonymous Holding LLC identity shielding.

### 2. Tone, Voice & Humanizer Rules
- **Peer-to-Peer Candor:** Write with direct, confident, and unfiltered insider knowledge. Never use patronizing moralizing, clinical lectures, or sanitized corporate jargon.
- **Strictly Banned AI Tell-Words:** Never use robotic filler words or clichés such as: `delve into`, `tapestry`, `furthermore`, `testament to`, `seamless`, `revolutionize`, `in conclusion`, `beacon`, `nestled`.
- **Dynamic Cadence (Burstiness):** Mix punchy 1-line statements with detailed technical breakdowns.

### 3. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Page Architecture
- **BLUF Answer (Bottom-Line-Up-Front):** The first 40–60 words immediately beneath every `<h1>` title must deliver a direct, quotation-ready summary of the page's core entity for instant AI Search citation (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search).
- **In-Site Internal Link Mesh:** Weave 4–8 context-rich internal links per page pointing to related service pages, university syllabus modules, and performance tools using keyword-rich anchor text.
- **Social Proof & Schema:** Every main page must integrate the 14-Creator Testimonials network (`TestimonialsSection`) and Blacklisted University Faculty Bio attributions (`AuthorBio`). Include valid JSON-LD schemas (`FAQPage`, `Service`, `Review`, `Course`, `SoftwareApplication`).
